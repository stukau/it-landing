Mobile App Development for iOS and Android

We develop apps for iOS and Android — from a first version that tests an idea to a product in the store. Our team's own apps are published in the App Store, so we have gone from idea to review and release more than once.

Discuss a project

What we do

  • MVP — a first version with one key feature done properly.
  • Customer apps — account areas, catalogs, booking, delivery.
  • AI features — receipt and screenshot recognition, voice input.
  • On-device data — when it matters that data never leaves the phone.
  • Monetization — subscriptions and in-app purchases.
  • Publishing — preparing for review and releasing to the App Store and Google Play.

When you need it

  • you have a product idea and need to test it on real users;
  • customers need an account area or booking always at hand;
  • you need device features: camera, voice, notifications, offline work;
  • it is unclear what pays off better — an app or a Telegram Mini App.

How the work goes

  1. We listen. You tell us about your business and the problem — we ask questions and dig in.
  2. We propose. What to do, how much it costs and how long it takes — broken down by stage.
  3. We build. With interim demos, so you see progress at every stage. The first working version is ready in 2 weeks.
  4. We launch and support. We deploy, train your team and stay in touch after launch.

Timeline and cost

The cost depends on the number of platforms, screens, the backend and integrations. We suggest starting with an MVP — one key feature, real integrations and minimal analytics — and growing it based on results. You get an estimate after a free consultation.

Questions and answers

What can you build in two weeks?

A first version with one key feature done properly: a working scenario, error handling and minimal analytics. Extra sections and secondary features are deliberately left for later stages.

Do you build for both iOS and Android?

Yes. Which platform to start with, and whether you need the second one right away, depends on your audience — we discuss it during the consultation.

An app or a Telegram Mini App?

A Mini App is faster and cheaper if your customers already use Telegram. An app is justified when you need offline mode, the camera, voice input, push notifications outside the messenger or a presence in the stores.

Will you help with publishing in the stores?

Yes. We prepare the materials, go through review and publish the app; after launch we stay in touch and ship updates.
