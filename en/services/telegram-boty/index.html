Questions and answers

How much does a Telegram bot cost?

Roughly from $200–600 for an informational bot with a menu to $3,000 and up for an AI-powered bot with integrations. The price depends on scenarios, integrations and how accurate the answers must be; you get an itemized estimate after we go through the task.

How fast can a bot be launched?

The first version covering the main scenario usually launches in two weeks. Complex integrations and AI are added in the following stages.

How is a custom bot different from one made in a bot builder?

A builder is fine for a simple menu and standard scenarios. Custom development is needed when you require integrations with your systems, non-standard logic, AI or a Mini App.

Bot, Mini App or mobile app — which one?

If your customers already use Telegram and you need booking, a catalog or an account area, a Mini App is usually faster and cheaper than an app. A standalone app is justified when you need offline mode, access to device features or a presence in the stores.

Can the bot be connected to a CRM?

Yes. The bot can create deals, update customer records and hand the conversation over to a manager together with the chat history.
