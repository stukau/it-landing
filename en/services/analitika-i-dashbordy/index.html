Data Analytics and Dashboards for Business

We bring data from different sources into one picture, so the owner can see what is happening in the business and decide by the numbers rather than by gut feeling.

Questions and answers

Where to start if the data is scattered across spreadsheets?

With one question you need answered every day — for example, how many leads came in yesterday and at what cost. We collect the sources for it and build the first dashboard, then add the rest.

Which metrics does an owner need?

About ten is usually enough: revenue, leads, conversion, average order value, acquisition cost, stock, receivables. The exact set depends on the business.

Do we need an expensive BI system for this?

Not always. For a small business a lightweight dashboard and a daily summary in Telegram are often enough. Heavy BI platforms are justified with many sources and many users.

Can we ask questions about our data in plain text?

Yes — with a language model connected to your data. It is important to limit it to verified sources so that it doesn't make numbers up.
