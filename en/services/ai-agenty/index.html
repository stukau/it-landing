AI Agent Development and Business Automation

We build AI agents — digital employees that process leads, advise customers, fill in your CRM and connect your services together. We start with one process and expand step by step.

Discuss a project

What we do

  • Lead processing — qualifying leads around the clock and handing them to a manager.
  • Customer support — answers from a knowledge base with escalation to a human.
  • Knowledge base (RAG) — the AI answers from your company's documents.
  • Documents — invoice recognition, contract drafts, deadline tracking.
  • Integrations — CRM, accounting systems, spreadsheets, messengers.
  • Process automation — on n8n, Make or custom code, depending on the task.
  • Error protection — the agent does not invent prices, deadlines or terms.

When you need it

  • managers spend time on repetitive questions and initial qualification;
  • leads outside working hours go unanswered;
  • company knowledge sits in scattered documents and newcomers take a long time to get up to speed;
  • data is copied between systems by hand.

  3. We build. With interim demos, so you see progress at every stage. The first working version is ready in 2 weeks.

Questions and answers

How is an AI agent different from a chatbot?

A chatbot follows predefined buttons and scenarios. An agent understands free text, chooses the action itself — answer, clarify, create a deal, hand over to a human — and works with your systems.

Which process should we automate first?

One with a lot of repetitive manual work and a clear outcome: initial lead processing, answers to frequent questions, moving data between systems. One process — one first version.

How do you stop an agent from making things up?

The agent answers only from verified sources, takes prices and terms from your systems rather than from memory, and hands the conversation to a human when unsure. Plus tests on real questions before launch.

Is it safe to share company data with AI?

It depends on the data and the requirements. Options include cloud models with contractual guarantees or open models on your own server — we choose based on how sensitive the data is.

How much does an AI agent cost?

From a few hundred dollars for automation on a ready-made platform to tens of thousands for a system integrated with accounting and its own model. Plus monthly costs for the model, server and support.
