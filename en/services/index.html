Services

Six directions — one goal: solve your business challenge through IT

Websites & Landing Pages

We build websites that do a job for the business: bring in leads, explain the product, and are easy for search engines and AI assistants to read. We start with a first working version and grow it as real data comes in.

Telegram Bots & AI Assistants

We build bots around a specific business scenario — from a simple menu and booking to an AI assistant that understands a customer's question, answers from your company documents and creates a deal in your CRM.

Mobile Applications

We develop apps for iOS and Android — from a first version that tests an idea to a product in the store. Our team's own apps are published in the App Store, so we have gone from idea to review and release more than once.

CRM & Automation

We set up and implement a CRM so that the team actually uses it and the business stops losing leads and customers. We automate the routine around sales: funnels, reminders, mailings and reports.

Analytics & Dashboards

We bring data from different sources into one picture, so the owner can see what is happening in the business and decide by the numbers rather than by gut feeling.

AI Agents & Business Automation

We build AI agents — digital employees that process leads, advise customers, fill in your CRM and connect your services together. We start with one process and expand step by step.
