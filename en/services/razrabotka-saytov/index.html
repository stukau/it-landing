Questions and answers

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page usually takes up to two weeks including copy and a lead form. A corporate site or an online store is built in stages: we launch a minimal working version first, then add sections and integrations.

Is SEO included?

The technical foundation is: heading structure, meta tags, structured data, sitemap, robots.txt, responsive layout and loading speed. Ongoing promotion — articles, links, keyword work — is discussed separately.

Can the website be connected to a CRM and messengers?

Yes. Form submissions can go straight to a CRM, a managers' Telegram chat or a spreadsheet, so they don't get lost in email.

What does preparing a site for AI search mean?

A set of technical steps — llms.txt, schema.org markup, a robots.txt open to AI crawlers and clearly structured copy — so that ChatGPT and other assistants can read the site and mention the company in their answers.
