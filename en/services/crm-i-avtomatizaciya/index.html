CRM Implementation and Sales Automation

We set up and implement a CRM so that the team actually uses it and the business stops losing leads and customers. We automate the routine around sales: funnels, reminders, mailings and reports.

Discuss a project

What we do

  • Choosing a CRM — for your processes and team size, not just “the most popular one”.
  • Sales funnels — stages, tasks and reminders without manual control.
  • Messenger integration — every conversation in one customer record.
  • Telephony — calls and recordings in the deal record.
  • Mailings — segments, sequences, winning customers back.
  • Automatic reports — a daily summary for the owner, for example in Telegram.
  • AI in CRM — automatic tags from conversations, call summaries.

When you need it

  • leads are tracked in spreadsheets and chats, and some of them get lost;
  • you bought a CRM, but managers don't fill it in;
  • it is unclear at which stage deals fall through;
  • the owner assembles a report by hand from several systems.

How the work goes

  1. We listen. You tell us about your business and the problem — we ask questions and dig in.
  2. We propose. What to do, how much it costs and how long it takes — broken down by stage.
  3. We build. With interim demos, so you see progress at every stage. The first working version is ready in 2 weeks.
  4. We launch and support. We deploy, train your team and stay in touch after launch.

Timeline and cost

The cost of implementation depends on the system chosen, the number of users, the number of integrations and the state of the data to be migrated. We start with one process — usually incoming leads — and expand step by step. You get an itemized estimate after a free review.

Questions and answers

Where does a CRM implementation start?

With a review of the current process: where leads come from, who handles them and how, and where they get lost. Only then do we choose a system and set up the funnel.

Why doesn't the team use the CRM, and what can be done?

Most often because the system adds work instead of removing it. What helps: deals created automatically from messengers and calls, a minimum of required fields, and training the team on real scenarios.

Can a CRM be connected to Telegram and WhatsApp?

Yes. Messenger conversations land in the customer record, and the manager replies from a single window.

Does a very small team need a CRM?

If there are a few leads a day handled by one person, a spreadsheet and a bot may be enough. A CRM pays off when leads grow, several managers appear or you need to see the whole funnel.
