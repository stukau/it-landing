Frequently asked questions

How much does development cost?

It depends on the task: an informational Telegram bot is roughly $200–600, an AI-powered bot with integrations starts at $3,000; websites, apps, CRM and analytics are estimated after a short discussion. You get an itemized estimate before work begins, and the consultation is free.

What does “MVP in 2 weeks” mean?

A first working version with one key feature done properly: a real scenario, error handling and minimal analytics. Secondary features are deliberately postponed and added as the product proves itself.

What do you need from us to start?

Tell us about the business and the problem. We ask questions, dig in and propose a solution with a price and timeline. You don't need to write a specification in advance.

Do you support the project after launch?

Yes. We deploy, train your team and stay in touch: fixing, improving and extending the solution.

Which languages do you work in?

English, Russian, Spanish and French.
